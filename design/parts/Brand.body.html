Lanes

One row per connection, an hour left to right. Read across for one service; read down for what everything was doing at a single moment. Absence is hatched, never merely grey.

The component matrix

One pip per component, grouped by the connection that reports it. A hundred and forty-eight things in the space of a paragraph — and a fault is visible before you have read a word.
