Chart system

One vocabulary,
three readers.

The same estate, asked three different questions. A developer wants the tail of a distribution at 14:04; a maintainer wants the shape of the whole estate; management wants to know whether the commitment held. Every chart below draws to the same rules, so a person who learns one has learned all of them.

The rules, in full
01A status hue means a state. Nothing else may wear one.
02Series hues carry identity, in fixed order, never sorted by value.
03Nominal is quiet. Saturation is reserved for what is wrong.
04Motion explains where a value came from, and never loops.
05A measure is shown against its limit, or not at all.
